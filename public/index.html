Clinical Reference

Diabetic Retinopathy Classification Scale

International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy Disease Severity Scale (ICDR)

0
No DR

Normal Retinal Architecture

No microaneurysms, hemorrhages, or exudates. Healthy vascular branching.

Annual Follow-up
1
Mild NPDR

Microaneurysms Only

Focal capillary wall outpouchings without macular edema or widespread lipid exudation.

Review in 6-12 Months
2
Moderate NPDR

Hemorrhages & Exudates

Microaneurysms, intraretinal dot-and-blot hemorrhages, and hard lipid exudates in 1-3 quadrants.

Consult in 3-6 Months
3
Severe NPDR

4:2:1 Clinical Criteria

>20 hemorrhages per quadrant, venous beading in 2+ quadrants, or IRMA in 1+ quadrant.

Urgent Specialist (2-4 wks)
4
Proliferative DR

Neovascularization (PDR)

Abnormal new blood vessels on disc (NVD) or retina (NVE), vitreous hemorrhage risk.

EMERGENCY (24-48 hrs)
